Alakaʻi Test Prep

The time squeeze

Listen: Ep 3

A one-page handout. Read it once, then practice it in Timed until it is automatic.

1. The two rules that never change

  • No guessing penalty on the SAT or the ACT. A blank is a guaranteed zero. A guess on a four-choice question is worth a quarter point on average, more once you cross out one wrong choice. Never leave a blank.
  • Every question is worth the same. The monster question at the end counts exactly as much as the easy one you skipped past. Collect the easy points first.

ACT Reading is 4 passages in 40 minutes: 10 minutes a passage, no exceptions. If a passage is eating you alive, answer what you can and go to the next one.

3. The two-pass method

  1. Pass 1: go in order. If you see the answer path in 10 seconds, do it. If you don't, pick your best guess, flag it, and move. Do not "just try for one more minute." Three of those minutes is two or three other questions you never saw.
  2. Pass 2: with whatever time is left, go back to the flags, hardest-looking last. You already have a guess down on every one of them, so you are only upgrading.
  3. Last 60 seconds: stop solving. Check that every question has an answer. Then stop.

4. Checkpoints

Look at the clock exactly twice, at the one-third and two-thirds marks, and ask one question: am I at or past the target question number? RW: question 9 at 10:40 and 18 at 21:20. Flag faster. Ahead? Slow down one notch; speed is not the problem.

5. Questions you will not have time for

  • Cross out any choice you know is wrong, then guess among what is left. Even one elimination changes the math.
  • Pick a letter for pure guesses and stay with it. Switching letters does not help and costs seconds.
  • On the digital SAT, the flag and the question grid are built in. Use them. On the ACT, flag it if you are testing online; on paper, circle the question number in the test booklet.
  • Do not go back and change an answer unless you found a specific reason. First instincts are right more often than second-guesses.

6. The week before

  • Two full timed sections, back to back, at the same hour as your real test.
  • Redo everything in your error log that is due.
  • The night before: pau. No new material. Sleep.
