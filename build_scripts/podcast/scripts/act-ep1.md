# ACT Notes Ep 1: How the ACT Works
cast: lehua, sarah, derek, pono, kaden
facts: Enhanced ACT (rolled out starting April 2025 online, September 2025 paper): English 50 questions in 35 minutes; Math 45 questions in 50 minutes; Reading 36 questions in 40 minutes; Science is optional, 40 questions in 40 minutes; Writing is optional, 40 minutes; core test without Science is about 2 hours 5 minutes of testing; each section scored 1 to 36; Composite is the average of English, Math and Reading rounded to the nearest whole number; Science is reported separately and a STEM score is reported when Science is taken; no guessing penalty; offered online or on paper at test centers; 70 dollars for English, Math and Reading, 75 with Science, more with Writing (act.org Current ACT Fees and Services page, checked 2026-10-16), fee waivers through the school counselor cover registration and score reports to colleges (act.org fee waivers page); scores and registration through a MyACT account; ACT provides a superscore from multiple test dates; class of 2025 national average Composite 19.4 (act.org/gradclass2025, checked 2026-10-16); UH Manoa is test-optional (manoa.hawaii.edu/admissions/freshman, checked 2026-10-16); UH Hilo considers official ACT or SAT scores for first-year applicants (hilo.hawaii.edu/admissions/freshman.php, checked 2026-10-16); Science score 1 to 36 appears on the score report when taken, STEM is the average of Math and Science (act.org Understanding Your Scores, checked 2026-10-16); colleges that consider scores accept either the SAT or the ACT; most colleges publish a middle 50 percent range for admitted or enrolled students; sections run English, Math, Reading, then Science (act.org); the pre-2025 ACT was 215 questions in 2 hours 55 minutes without Writing, so the enhanced test is shorter with fewer questions (act.org enhancements page); on paper bring sharpened No. 2 pencils with erasers, no mechanical pencils, and a watch with no alarm is allowed (act.org/the-act/id, checked 2026-10-16); online testing has a countdown timer embedded on screen (ACT online testing tools guide, doe.sd.gov OnlineACTtools.pdf, checked 2026-10-16); Kona Costco closes 7 PM Saturday (cast.json places)
