The other three are informational, nonfiction about things like science or music history. And one of those is a pair of shorter passages that you compare. Each passage has a bunch of questions. So the whole section is really four ten-minute games.
KADEN: Four ten-minute games. Okay, I can do ten minutes.
SARAH: That's the plan. Ten minutes a passage, no exceptions. That's piece one. Piece two is order. Piece three is how you read.
SARAH: Order first. ACT's own prep guide says to answer the easy questions first, skip the hard ones, and go back to them if you have time, as long as you're still in that section. So inside Reading, you don't have to go in order. Some people go straight through. Some people start with the kind of passage they're best at, so they bank easy points early. Try both on practice and pick one. Don't decide on test day.
LEHUA: And the ten-minute rule. What happens at minute nine?
SARAH: At minute nine you start finishing. If you're still stuck on two questions, you put your best guesses in, and you go to the next passage. The next passage might be your best one. Don't let a bad passage steal time from a good one.
LEHUA: Okay, how you read. This is the big argument. Read the questions first, or skim the passage first?
SARAH: Both work. Here's the honest answer. Questions first means you know what you're hunting for, and the app's strategy list starts there: read the question first. Skim first means you have a map before the questions hit you. Some people do better one way, some the other. Do two practice passages each way and time yourself. Pick the one where you finish with better accuracy.
DEREK: Pick by the numbers, not by how it feels.
SARAH: Thank you, Derek.
SARAH: But either way, one rule doesn't change. Go back to the line. When a question gives you a line number, or names a detail, you go back and reread it. Plus a few lines before and after. Never answer from memory. The app warns you about this exact thing. Answering from memory and picking a choice that uses passage words with the wrong meaning.
