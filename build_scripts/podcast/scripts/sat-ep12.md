PONO: Okay, listen up, this one is important. Most students practice like this. Do twenty questions, look at the score, feel good or feel bad, close the app. That's like shooting free throws and only counting the makes. The questions you got right didn't teach you much. The ones you missed are where the points are.
PONO: So here's the habit. Every miss gets sorted into one of three buckets. Careless. Concept. Or time. And each bucket has a different fix.
DEREK: Careless means you knew how to do it and slipped. A sign, a subtraction, a misread word. Concept means you didn't know the rule or the method. Time means you rushed or guessed because the clock was running.
LEHUA: And the app asks you, right?
PONO: Right. When you miss a drill question, the app asks, "Why did you miss it?" and gives you three buttons. Careless. Didn't know it. Rushed. Be honest. Nobody sees it but you.

DEREK: Here's a real-sounding miss. I'll read it twice. "The question was, what is fifteen percent of eighty? The student wrote point one five times eighty and got fourteen. The correct answer is twelve." Which bucket?
DEREK: Again. "What is fifteen percent of eighty? The student wrote point one five times eighty and got fourteen. The correct answer is twelve." Careless, concept, or time?

LEHUA: And we're back. Kaden?
KADEN: Careless. Because the setup was right. Point one five times eighty is exactly what you're supposed to do. They just multiplied wrong.
DEREK: Correct. Point one five times eighty is twelve. Ten percent of eighty is eight, five percent is four, eight plus four is twelve. The student knew the method and slipped on the arithmetic. In the app, that trap is called calculation slip.
PONO: Now, what if the student had written eighty divided by fifteen?
KADEN: Then that's concept. They didn't know how percents work.

PONO: There you go. Same miss, three different causes, three different fixes.
LEHUA: So what are the fixes?
DEREK: Careless gets a check. Slow down on the last step, and check backward. Twelve divided by eighty is point one five. That's three seconds. Concept gets studied. Open the Strategies tab for that skill, read the steps, and drill ten of that skill. Time gets a pacing fix, which is episode three. Two passes, flag it if you can't see the path in about ten seconds, and keep the checkpoints.
KADEN: So if I mark everything careless, that's bad?
DEREK: If you mark everything careless, you're probably hiding concept misses. Careless feels better. It says, "I'm smart, I just slipped." But if the same skill keeps showing up as careless, it's probably a concept.
PONO: Now here's the part people skip. The redo. The app puts every miss in your error log on the dashboard, with the trap name. Three days later, the item comes back due for a redo. Not the same day. Three days. If you can get it right three days later, you actually learned it. In the app, a skill only counts as Mastered after you get a redo right.
KADEN: Why three days? Why not tomorrow?
PONO: Because tomorrow you still remember the answer. Three days later, you have to remember the method. That's the real test.
LEHUA: And the weekly plan?
DEREK: On the dashboard. You told the app how many minutes a day you have when you set it up. It multiplies by seven and builds your week, weighted toward your weakest skills.
