A quadratic can be written three ways, and each one hands you a different answer for free. Your job is to ask what they want, then pick the form that shows it.
DEREK: Vertex form looks like a, times the quantity x minus h, squared, plus k. The vertex is the point h comma k. If the number in front is negative, the U is upside down, so k is the maximum. If it's positive, k is the minimum.
DEREK: Factored form looks like x minus p, times x minus q. Those p and q are the roots, the x-values where it crosses zero. Watch the sign. x minus four means the root is positive four. x plus one means the root is negative one.
DEREK: And standard form, a x squared plus b x plus c. The c by itself is the y-intercept.
