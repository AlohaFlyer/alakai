facts: Free official full-length practice tests are in Bluebook (College Board, bluebook.collegeboard.org); the digital SAT is 2 hr 14 min of testing plus one 10-minute break between Reading and Writing and Math (College Board); Desmos is built into Bluebook (College Board); the PSAT/NMSQT is given by schools in October (fall 2026 window October 1 to 30; students do not register through College Board, schools and districts choose whether and when to offer it; satsuite.collegeboard.org/psat-nmsqt/test-dates, checked 2026-10-16), and students enter National Merit by taking the PSAT/NMSQT in the junior year (BigFuture National Merit Scholarship page, bigfuture.collegeboard.org/scholarships/national-merit-scholarship;
