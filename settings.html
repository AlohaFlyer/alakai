Disclosures

What stays here. Alakaʻi Test Prep stores everything you do on this device only: your answers, scores, timing, podcast progress, your onboarding profile, your name and any email addresses you type. We cannot see any of it.

The one thing that is sent. When you finish onboarding, the app sends one anonymous message with exactly two fields, your school name and your grade level, so we can see which schools use this tool. It looks like this: {"school":"hpa","grade":"11"}. No name, no email, no scores, no device ID, no cookie. It is sent once.

Progress reports. Reports open in your own email app, addressed to you with your parent or teacher in CC if you entered one. They go through your mail, not ours. A copy is kept in your local Sent log.

Young students. We collect no personal information from anyone. The app works the same for every age.

About the border pattern

The frame is solid by default. Seven optional patterns are available above under Border pattern; each is an original geometric pattern in the manner of ʻohe kāpala, the carved bamboo stamps used to print kapa and decorate lauhala. Nothing in it is sacred imagery, and nothing is copied from a petroglyph, a family kapa, or a quilt.

KohalaLow triangles for the Kohala mountain and one tall peak for Mauna Kea, under a diagonal hatch for Kīpuʻupuʻu, the cold wind-driven rain of Waimea. Pili grass ticks along the bottom.
LauhalaA plaited lattice, with an ʻōhiʻa lehua cluster where the strands cross, for the Kohala forest.
PanioloStacked chevrons for the ridgelines above town and a ranch-fence rhythm of posts and rails, for paniolo country.
Kaula, Lariat, Saddle stitch, HorseshoeRope-work and ranch motifs from Waimea's paniolo tradition: a twisted rope, a lasso coil, a stitched strap, a marching horseshoe.
